How it's calculated

Monthly instalment

Loan = Purchase Price × (1 − Down Payment %). The instalment is the standard fully amortizing payment: P × r × (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n − 1), where r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of months. A 0% rate is just the loan divided by the number of months.

Interest and principal

Each month, interest is the outstanding balance times the monthly rate. The rest of the instalment reduces principal. The last month pays off whatever balance remains, so the schedule ends at zero.

Debt servicing

TDSR = (This Instalment + Other Monthly Debt) / Gross Monthly Income. The ceiling used here is 55% of gross income. The loan that fits is the principal whose instalment, at the same rate and tenure, equals Income × 55% − Other Debt.
