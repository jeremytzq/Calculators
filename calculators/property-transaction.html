How it's calculated

Gross sales & cash proceeds

For HDB: Gross Sales Proceeds = Selling Price − Outstanding Loan − Total CPF Used, with Gross Cash Proceeds equal to that figure. For private property: Gross Sales Proceeds = Selling Price − Outstanding Loan, then Gross Cash Proceeds = Gross Sales Proceeds − CPF Refund. Either way, the CPF used (with accrued interest) by every seller is refunded back to their CPF account before any cash is released — it's the same total deduction either way, just grouped differently.

Seller Stamp Duty (SSD)

Applies if you sell within a few years of purchase, on the selling price: 16% within 1 year, 12% within 1–2 years, 8% within 2–3 years, 4% within 3–4 years, and no SSD beyond 4 years — the current rates for residential property bought on or after 20 Feb 2010.

Agent fee

Agent Fee = Selling Price × Rate, optionally plus 9% GST on the commission. Turn off auto-calculate to enter a negotiated flat fee instead.

Net cash proceeds

Net Cash Proceeds = Gross Cash Proceeds − Total Expenses — the bottom-line amount you walk away with, across the whole transaction. The payment schedule on the right breaks that same total into what's actually received or paid out at each stage: for HDB, both the option fee and exercise fee are paid directly to the seller as they're received; for private property, only the option fee is — the exercise deposit is held by the buyer's lawyer and only released at completion, so it isn't treated as "already received" beforehand.
