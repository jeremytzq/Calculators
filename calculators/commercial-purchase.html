Commercial rules, not residential ones. ABSD does not apply, and CPF cannot fund the purchase. Stamp duty uses the non-residential schedule, capped at 4%. GST is cash on top when the seller is GST-registered.

How it's calculated

Buyer's stamp duty

Non-residential rates: 1% on the first S$180,000, 2% on the next S$180,000, 3% on the next S$640,000, and 4% on the rest. There is no further step-up, and no ABSD.

Cash required

Down Payment + GST + Stamp Duty + Legal + Agent + Renovation. GST is Price × GST % when you mark it applicable, and it is not added to the loan. The loan itself is Price × Loan-to-Value.

Tenure and instalment

The tenure used is the shorter of the tenure you enter and the remaining lease. The instalment is the standard amortizing payment on that shorter term.

Yield and cash-on-cash

Gross Yield = Annual Rent / Price. Net Yield = (Annual Rent − Annual Operating Costs) / Price. Cash-on-Cash = (Net Operating Income − Annual Instalments) / Cash Required.
