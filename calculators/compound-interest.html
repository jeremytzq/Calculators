How it's calculated

Effective monthly rate

Your nominal annual rate is converted to an effective monthly rate based on the compounding frequency you chose: (1 + rate/n)^(n/12) − 1, where n is the number of compounding periods per year. This lets contributions (always monthly) and compounding (any frequency) combine correctly.

Month-by-month growth

Each month, your contribution is added (at the start or end of the month, per your setting), then the balance grows by the effective monthly rate. This is simulated one month at a time rather than using a single closed-form formula, so the year-by-year breakdown is exact.

Total interest earned

Total Interest = Final Balance − Total Contributions, where total contributions is your initial principal plus every monthly contribution made along the way (no growth assumed on the contributions themselves — the interest figure captures all of the growth).
