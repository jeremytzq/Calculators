How it's calculated

ROI = (Final Value − Initial Investment) / Initial Investment × 100

Net Profit = Final Value − Initial Investment

Annualized Return (CAGR) = ((Final Value / Initial Investment) ^ (1 / Years) − 1) × 100, shown when you provide an investment period.
