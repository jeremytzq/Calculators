How it's calculated

Mortgage payment

M = L × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where L is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of monthly payments.

Net operating income (NOI)

NOI = Effective Gross Income − Operating Expenses, excluding mortgage payments and capital expenditures.

Cap rate

Cap Rate = NOI / Purchase Price × 100

Cash-on-cash return

Cash-on-Cash = Annual Cash Flow / Total Cash Invested × 100, where cash flow is NOI minus annual mortgage payments.

DSCR (debt service coverage ratio)

DSCR = NOI / Annual Mortgage Payments — lenders typically want this above 1.20–1.25.

Total ROI & annualized return (IRR)

At sale, the property's value grows by the appreciation rate, the loan balance is paid down via amortization, and selling costs are deducted to get net sale proceeds. Rent and expenses grow each year by their own growth rates to produce a year-by-year cash flow. Total ROI compares total profit (net sale proceeds + cumulative cash flow − cash invested) to cash invested. Annualized return is the true IRR of the full cash flow stream — the initial outlay, each year's cash flow, and the final sale proceeds — solved numerically, so it reflects the actual timing of your money rather than a simple average.
